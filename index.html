The method

Learn the language you’ll actually say.

Built around conversation, memory, and tiny daily wins. You learn the phrase, hear it, use it, then meet it again before your brain decides to throw it away.

01 · LISTEN

Train your ear first.

Native French pronunciation is attached to every useful phrase. Listen before you overthink the spelling.

02 · SPEAK

Useful from day one.

Coffee shops, introductions, directions, travel, friends. Start with conversations you can picture yourself having.

03 · REMEMBER

Review at the right moment.

Smart repetition brings words back just before they fade, so your practice stays small while your vocabulary keeps growing.
